Recipe for Fried plantain

Frying sweet plantains (dodo) takes just a few simple steps for a crispy outside and soft, caramelized center.

Ingredients

  • 2 to 3 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots or mostly black for a sweeter flavor)
  • ½ cup vegetable, canola, or coconut oil (for shallow frying)
  • Pinch of salt (optional, to balance the sweetness)

Instructions

  1. Prep the plantains: Cut off both ends of the plantains. Score the peel lengthwise with a knife without cutting deep into the flesh, then peel the skin off.
  2. Slice: Cut the plantains diagonally or into round discs about 1/2-inch to 3/4-inch thick so they cook evenly. Toss with a light pinch of salt if desired.
  3. Heat the oil: Pour about 1/2 inch of oil into your skillet and warm it over medium heat.
  4. Fry the first side: Carefully place the plantain slices into the hot oil in a single layer. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom turns golden brown and caramelized.
  5. Flip and finish: Use a spatula to flip the slices over. Fry for another 2 minutes until both sides are evenly browned. If they brown too fast, lower the heat to medium-low.
  6. Drain and serve: Remove the plantains and place them on a paper towel-lined plate to soak up excess oil.
