Recipe for Potatoes Porriage

Here is a simple and savory Nigerian-style Irish potato porridge recipe

Ingredients

  • Potatoes: 3 large Irish potatoes (peeled and cut into small chunks)
  • Palm Oil: 1/2 to 3/4 cup
  • Base & Aromatics: 2 medium red bell peppers, 4 fresh tomatoes, 1 medium onion (chopped), and fresh chili or scotch bonnet peppers to taste
  • Protein: 3 medium smoked fish or mackerel (or substitute with cooked beef, goat meat, or sardines)
  • Flavor & Seasoning: 2 to 3 seasoning cubes (Knorr or Maggi), 1 heaped tablespoon of ground crayfish, salt to taste
  • Vegetables: 1 cup of chopped pumpkin leaves (ugu), scent leaves, or spinach
  • Water: About 1 cup or just enough to cover the potatoes

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes: Wash the peeled potatoes, place them into a clean pot, and add just enough water to reach about an inch above the potatoes. Add your seasoning cubes, salt, and half of the chopped onions. Cook on medium heat until tender.
  2. Build the base: While the potatoes cook, heat the palm oil in a separate pan or right into a flavor base (or you can add the palm oil, crayfish, and blended peppers directly into the pot depending on your preferred style).
  3. Combine and mash: Once the potatoes are soft, mash a few pieces against the side of the pot with your cooking spoon; this naturally thickens the porridge.
  4. Add palm oil and spices: Stir in the palm oil, ground crayfish, blended scotch bonnets, and smoked fish or cooked meat. Allow it to simmer together for 3 to 5 minutes so the flavors meld.
  5. Add greens: Toss in your sliced spinach or pumpkin leaves (ugu). Stir well and let it simmer for 1 to 2 minutes using the residual heat, then turn off the stove
