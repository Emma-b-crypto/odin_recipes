Recipe For Jollof Rice

Here are easy steps to make the classic Nigerian Party Jollof

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice (or Basmati rice)
  • 4 Roma tomatoes (or fresh plum tomatoes)
  • 2 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 1–2 Scotch bonnet peppers (habanero/atarodo)
  • 1 large onion
  • ½ cup vegetable oil or olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3 cups meat stock (chicken, beef, or goat stock)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 bouillon cubes (Maggi or Knorr)
  • Salt for taste

Instructions

  1. Blend and Reduce the Pepper Mix
    • Blend your red bell peppers, plum tomatoes, scotch bonnets, onions, garlic, and ginger until smooth.
    • Pour the mixture into a pot and boil or simmer it for 35 to 40 minutes until most of the excess water evaporates.
  2. Prepare the Rice
    • Wash your long-grain parboiled rice thoroughly with warm or hot water to remove surface starch so the grains stay separate.
    • Alternatively, let it sit in hot water for about 3 minutes, then rinse with cold water.
  3. Fry the Stew Base
    • Heat vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat (using oil previously used to fry your chicken or meat adds deep flavor).
    • Add sliced onions and sauté until fragrant.
    • Stir in the tomato paste and fry for about 10 minutes until it darkens slightly and loses its sour taste.
    • Add your reduced pepper mix, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, bouillon cubes, and white pepper. Fry everything together for 15 to 20 minutes until the oil rises to the top.
  4. Combine Stock and Rice
    • Pour in your meat stock and let it bubble together with the fried stew base for 8 to 10 minutes.
    • Add the washed rice and stir gently so every grain is coated in the red sauce.
    • Ensure the liquid level sits just at or slightly above the top of the rice—do not add too much water.
  5. Steam and Cook
    • Cover the pot tightly with aluminum foil paper (to trap the steam) and place the pot lid securely on top.
    • Cook on low-to-medium heat for about 30 to 40 minutes.
    • For a smoky party flavor, you can let the bottom catch or burn slightly near the end, or cook on low heat.
  6. Finish and Serve
    • Open the pot, add a pat of butter, fresh sliced tomatoes, and extra onions, then stir gently or fluff the rice.
    • Let it rest off the heat for 10 to 15 minutes before serving.
